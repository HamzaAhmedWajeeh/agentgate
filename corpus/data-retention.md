# Data retention

Synthetic document describing the fictional Northwind Mutual retention schedule.

## Retention periods

Transaction records are retained for seven years from the date of the transaction. Support
correspondence is retained for two years. Authentication logs are retained for ninety days.
Anything not named in this schedule defaults to the shortest period that satisfies the
applicable obligation, rather than to the longest.

## Deletion on request

A customer may request erasure of data not subject to a statutory retention obligation. The
request is completed within thirty days. Where part of the data must be retained, the response
states which categories were retained and under which obligation, rather than refusing the
request as a whole.

## Backups

Backups are excluded from immediate deletion and expire on their own retention cycle of
thirty-five days. This exclusion is disclosed in the privacy notice. An erasure that claims to
cover backups it cannot reach is worse than one that states the limit plainly.

## Access logging

Every access to a restricted record is logged with the accessing identity, the timestamp, and
the stated purpose. The log is append-only and is itself retained for two years.
