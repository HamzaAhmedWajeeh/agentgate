# Complaints handling

Synthetic document describing the fictional Northwind Mutual complaints procedure.

## Acknowledgement

A complaint is acknowledged within two working days. The acknowledgement names the person
handling it and the date by which a substantive response is due.

## Common complaint categories

The three categories seen most often are delays in refund processing, charges the customer did
not recognise, and difficulty reaching a human. The third has been the fastest growing for four
consecutive quarters and is the one least visible in transaction data, because it produces no
failed transaction to count.

## Substantive response

A substantive response is due within eight weeks. It states the outcome, the reasoning, and the
customer's right to escalate to the external ombudsman. A response that omits the escalation
route is not a substantive response, regardless of how thorough the rest of it is.

## Root cause linkage

Where a complaint matches an entry in the incident register, the complaint record links to it.
Complaints that cluster without a matching incident are the ones worth reading: they indicate a
failure nothing is currently detecting.
