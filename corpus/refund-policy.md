# Refund policy

Synthetic document. Northwind Mutual is a fictional organisation invented for this repository.
No real customer, client, or institution is described anywhere in this corpus.

## Eligibility window

A customer may request a refund within thirty calendar days of the original charge. Requests
made after thirty days are reviewed individually and may be granted where a service failure is
documented in the incident register.

## Partial refunds

Where a subscription has been partially consumed, the refund is prorated to the day. Prorating
is calculated from the activation timestamp, not from the invoice date, because the two differ
whenever a customer activates late.

## Non-refundable charges

Setup fees and third-party pass-through charges are not refundable. This exclusion must be
stated on the invoice at the time of purchase; where it was not stated, the exclusion does not
apply and the charge is refunded in full.

## Escalation

A refund above five thousand units requires a second approver. The second approver may not be
the person who handled the original request. This separation exists because the control being
enforced is against a single actor completing a payout unilaterally, not against error.
